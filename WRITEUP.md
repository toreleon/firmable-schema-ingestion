**Runtime agent.** Eight stages inspect bytes, choose a parser, profile training rows, propose a mapping, execute withheld rows, critique/revise, revalidate and present approval evidence. Proposal and critique are separate model calls. Errors trigger bounded repair; fingerprints/checkpoints retain completed work across failures. A seventh source supplies raw bytes and acquisition metadata, not a mapping. One engine executes all configs; unsupported shapes stop for adapter review. The fixed 0.8 field-confidence floor cannot be lowered by the agent. Human review sees raw examples, rationales, errors, unmapped fields, semantic risks and licences, and approves exact mapping hashes.

**Evidence and hard source.** Ingestion preserves source claims independently, with traceable snapshot/row identities, licence, extractor/config identity and distinct observation/ingestion times. **Identity policy.** Versioned pairwise links cite raw-record endpoints, canonical ABN/ACN key, rule score and evidence. Threshold **0.98** admits checksum-valid exact ABN (score 0.995) or ACN (0.985), with corroborating normalized names and compatible subject/type/capacity. Rule scores are not precision estimates. Only explicit corporate own-capacity ABN/ACN co-occurrence bridges namespaces; suffix inference and name-only links abstain. Employees, trustees, conflicting identifiers and unsafe compound parties are refused. Incompatible supported subjects cannot be unioned into one profile. Rejected endpoints and dependent bridges remain quarantined. Parent contracts supply no ownership evidence, so ownership edges remain absent.

**Link evaluation.** The submitted matcher has **50 explicit verdicts, 50 correct, precision 100% at threshold 0.98**. The sample reuses 44 previously checked raw-record pairs and adds six seeded current proposals. The user confirmed human verification of all 50 verdicts. Final attribution is 50 human; earlier AI-prepared assessments and per-item reasoning are retained as provenance. This is a targeted development sample, not independent population precision; it does not establish that every proposed link is correct. `outputs/audit_summary_submission.json` records the denominator, threshold and reviewer mix. Approval is recorded separately from correctness.

**Dataset evaluation remains incomplete.** Of the frozen 20-item shortlist sample, 12 are positive, three negative and **five inconclusive**. Unavailable/empty source responses and missing PDF/API records remain evidence gaps, not negative labels.

**Profiles and withdrawal.** Each field preserves confidence, winning provenance, alternatives and selection reasons. Reconciliation ranks source reliability, distinct-source support, mapping confidence and publication proxy, with stable tie-breaks; duplicate rows are not independent corroboration. Conflicting field confidence is capped at 0.7. Current selected profiles contain no conflicting fields; conflict retention is tested. Removing QBCC changes 50 profiles and loses 99 fields; removing the operator register changes 46 and loses 46. These are fixed-membership counterfactuals: production withdrawal must also retract links and rebuild graph membership.
